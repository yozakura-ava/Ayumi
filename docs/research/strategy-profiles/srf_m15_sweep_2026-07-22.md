**1. All 10 walk-forward runs verdict = `no-go`.**
Reasons vary by combo:
- `VolatilitySqueeze` EURUSD/GBPUSD/XAUUSD M15: mean PF<1.0 (0.74-0.79) → unprofitable
- `VolatilityRegimeBreakout` EURUSD/GBPUSD M15: trade count too low (5-6 total across 5 windows) → insufficient sample
- `VolatilityRegimeBreakout` XAUUSD M15: mean PF 3.38 but CV=1.20 (inconsistent across regimes) → no-go
- `KillzoneMomentum` XAUUSD M15: mean PF 0.97 (barely break-even) → no-go
- `SRMRPlus` XAUUSD M15: mean PF 1.77 but only 1/5 windows pass PF≥1.3 with ≥20 trades
- `SRMRPlus` EURUSD M15: 3/5 windows pass internal go_nogo (mean PF 1.75) but CV=0.56 just over threshold
- `SRMRPlus` GBPUSD M15: mean PF 1.43 but 0/5 windows clear PF≥1.3 ≥20-trade bar

**2. Best candidates (close to go, need further work):**
- `SRMRPlus EURUSD M15` — 3/5 internal passes, mean PF 1.75, WR 50%, only failure is PF CV=0.56 (just over 0.5)
- `SRMRPlus XAUUSD M15` — mean PF 1.77, 2/5 passes, smoke PF 2.95 — fresh breakouts worth more bars
- `VolatilityRegimeBreakout XAUUSD M15` — mean PF 3.38 (highest of the sweep) but trade-starved (only 39 total)

**3. Dead strategies / pairs from smoke:**
- `KillzoneMomentum` on EURUSD, GBPUSD (0 trades — FX-incompatible by design)
- `VolatilityRegimeBreakout` on EURUSD/GBPUSD M15 produces only 1-2 trades over 5000 bars (needs config tuning)

**4. Threshold change effect.** Raising `MIN_TRADES_PER_WINDOW` from 15 → 20 disqualifies more windows — every single window in this sweep has exactly 20 trades (likely the smoke cap) so the threshold change is effectively binding on most evaluations. The mean trades/window field documents this saturation.

**5. Sanity:** Note the `_compute_metrics` helper caps profit_factor at 10.0 (not ∞) when there are no losses, and the walk-forward runner further caps at PF_CAP=99.0. No Infinity/NaN propagated.
